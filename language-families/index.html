    How to read this map

    Sources & method

    What the areas mean

    Language families group languages by demonstrated common inheritance. The shapes are generalized scholarly distributions—not borders, populations, official-language status, or exclusive occupancy. Overlap is expected.

    Two imperfect time views

    Traditional / homeland uses contact-era areas in the Americas and Australia and Atlas-era proxies elsewhere. Atlas-era contemporary usually reflects the source atlas around 2007; it is not a live 2026 survey.

      Important limits

      Points locate documented languages or lineages but do not imply territory. Unmapped land means “no polygon in this source,” not “no language.” Pidgins, mixed and artificial languages, speech registers, and unclassifiable or unattested records are excluded from the genealogical fill.
